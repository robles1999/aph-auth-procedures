Required Preparation Steps

1

Review referral communications history. Continue with verification if needed.

2

Make sure a valid RX (Prescription/Referral) is in the system.

If a valid RX is not on file, follow established process to request an updated RX from the provider.

3

Ensure coverage eligibility by way of Registration (RTE), Passport, or Insurance Portal.

If the RTE is used, it is a good idea to also review the Response History to check for any alerts or issues with the insurance that may require follow-up.

4

Add a Rehab Insurance Summary note in the referral communications section

  • SmartPhrase 👉 REHABINSURANCESUMMARY
5

Complete the Rehab Insurance Summary form using the portal information

  • Access the Payer Portal to gather the patient's insurance details
  • Use the information retrieved to fill out each item of the Rehab Insurance Summary form
